Course overview

Welcome to FL248

English for Tourism provides learners with specialized English vocabulary about tourism, focusing on topics such as knowledge about types of resorts, tourism activities, tourism management activities. tourism, travel activities, practical language to guide visitors, means of transportation, local and international special holidays and festivals. Students can learn about technical terms related to the above topics; From there, students can apply the knowledge they have learned in basic communication situations. The program also gives children the opportunity to experience real-life communication with foreigners and carry out small projects to create community service products.

Grading

Score calculation

  • Grading components and final test scores will be marked on a scale of 10 (0 to 10), rounded to one decimal place.
  • Course score is the sum of all the components of the evaluation multiplied by the corresponding weight. The course score is marked on a scale of 10 and rounded to one decimal place, then it is converted to A-B-C-D score and score on a scale of 4 under the academic regulations of the University.
